UI2-1 - Macro Analysis

Write the macroeconomic context that affected the target company during the analysis period.

Things to include in a macro analysis:
General macroeconomic factors: GDP growth or forecasts during the time period, inflation, and bank interest rates for all areas of the globe where the company operates.
Industry-specific macro factors: margin pressure, sector-level demand, and whether the company operates in multiple geographies or sectors.
